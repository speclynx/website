Manifesto

A note from the maintainers

Why we built SpecLynx, what we believe about API tooling, and where we are taking it.

We are writing this note now, in the open, like everything else we do. Until it lands here, the short version: we spent years maintaining the tooling this ecosystem runs on, we watched specs multiply while tools stayed single-purpose and lossy, and we are building the one toolchain we always wanted to exist.
